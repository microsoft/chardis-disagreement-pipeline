- describes the taste, quality, or preparation of food.
- discusses the presentation or appearance of food.
- includes opinions or evaluations about the food served.

- mentions the behavior or attitude of the staff.
- refers to specific names of the staff members and interactions with them.
- includes comments about the staff's service quality.
- describes the staff's knowledge or expertise regarding the menu or services.
- includes any comments about the staff's performance or conduct.

- mentions the duration of service, such as how long it took to receive food or drinks.
