# Confidence

After providing your annotation, assign a confidence rating from 1 to 5 based on how certain 
you are in your judgment. 

Use the following scale:

1: Very Low Confidence  
 - The text is highly ambiguous, incomplete, contradictory, or confusing. 
 - You are unsure how the definitions apply.

2: Low Confidence  
 - The text is somewhat unclear or partially ambiguous. 
 - You can apply the definitions, but with notable uncertainty.

3: Moderate Confidence  
 - The text is mostly clear. 
 - Some ambiguity exists, but the definitions can be applied reasonably well.

4: High Confidence  
 - The text is clear and aligns well with the definitions.  
 - Only minor uncertainty remains.

5: Very High Confidence  
 - The text is very clear and unambiguous.  
 - You can apply the definitions with full certainty and no hesitation.

Return only the numeric value (1–5).
